# Privacy Policy

**Sara Riley Bot**
**Effective Date:** 28/08/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sara Riley Bot ("the Bot", "Service", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and otherwise processes information when the Bot is used within Discord servers or through its associated administrative dashboard.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through Sara Riley Bot and its administrative dashboard.

Discord independently processes information through its own services. Discord's processing is governed by Discord's own privacy policies and terms.

## 2. Information We May Process

The specific information processed depends on which features a Discord server enables and how the Service is configured.

### Discord Account and Server Information

We may process identifiers and information supplied through Discord, including:

* Discord user IDs;
* Discord usernames and display names;
* Discord server (guild) IDs;
* Channel IDs;
* Message IDs;
* Role information;
* Relevant server permissions; and
* Other Discord metadata necessary to provide moderation functionality.

We generally rely on Discord's identifiers rather than requiring users to create separate accounts with the Bot.

### Moderation Information

When moderation features are used, we may process information including:

* Warnings;
* Warning reasons;
* Moderator identifiers;
* User identifiers;
* Kick actions;
* Ban actions;
* Channel lock and unlock actions;
* Moderation timestamps; and
* Related audit information.

This information is used to maintain moderation history and provide server administrators with accountability and management tools.

### Message Event Information

Where message logging is enabled by a server administrator, the Bot may process information associated with messages that are edited or deleted.

This may include:

* Message content;
* Previous message content;
* Updated message content;
* Discord user ID;
* Username or display information;
* Channel ID;
* Message ID; and
* Relevant timestamps.

The Bot does not necessarily store every message sent within a server. Message information may instead be processed or stored when required for enabled functionality such as deletion and edit logging.

The exact behavior depends on the Bot's current configuration and technical implementation.

### Configuration Information

We may store server-specific settings, including:

* Whether particular logging features are enabled;
* Which channel is designated for moderation or event logs;
* Warning configuration;
* Moderation settings;
* Dashboard configuration; and
* Other server preferences necessary to operate enabled features.

### Dashboard and Security Information

When the administrative dashboard is used, we may process information necessary to authenticate users, authorize access, maintain security, and record configuration changes.

Depending on the deployed version of the Service, this may include authentication information, session information, Discord identifiers, administrative actions, and security-related logs.

Passwords, Discord bot tokens, API keys, and similar secrets should not be intentionally stored in ordinary moderation or audit records.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information processed through the Service may be used to:

* Provide moderation functionality;
* Execute authorized moderation commands;
* Maintain warning histories;
* Record moderation actions;
* Display relevant user information to authorized moderators;
* Log deleted or edited messages when enabled;
* Lock and unlock channels;
* Maintain server configuration;
* Operate the administrative dashboard;
* Diagnose technical problems;
* Prevent abuse or unauthorized access;
* Protect the security and integrity of the Service;
* Respond to support requests; and
* Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

We do not use message content processed through moderation logging to serve targeted advertising.

## 4. Legal Bases

Where laws such as the European Union or United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation apply, personal data must be processed under an appropriate legal basis.

Depending on the circumstances, processing may be based on legitimate interests, contractual necessity, compliance with legal obligations, consent where specifically required, or another lawful basis available under applicable law.

Server operators may independently have obligations concerning their configuration and use of moderation and logging functionality.

## 5. Server Administrators and Moderators

Discord server owners and administrators determine whether to add the Bot to their server and may control which features are enabled.

Server administrators may therefore influence which information the Service processes within their server.

Server owners and administrators are responsible for ensuring that their use of logging and moderation features complies with applicable laws, Discord policies, and any disclosures or community rules applicable to their server.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed or made available in limited circumstances, including:

### Authorized Server Personnel

Moderation records, warnings, logs, and configuration information may be accessible to authorized server owners, administrators, or moderators.

### Service Providers

Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers necessary to host, secure, maintain, or operate the Service.

Such providers process information according to their applicable agreements and privacy obligations.

### Discord

The Service communicates with Discord through Discord's APIs and infrastructure. Information transmitted through Discord may therefore be processed by Discord according to Discord's own policies.

### Legal and Safety Requirements

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to:

* Comply with applicable law or valid legal process;
* Protect the rights or security of the Service;
* Investigate fraud or abuse;
* Address security incidents; or
* Protect users or others where required or permitted by law.

## 7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to operational, security, backup, dispute-resolution, and legal requirements.

Moderation records may be retained while a server continues to use the Service because historical warnings and audit records are part of the Bot's moderation functionality.

Where technically and legally appropriate, information may be deleted or anonymized after it is no longer required.

Server removal does not necessarily result in immediate deletion of all associated information.

## 8. Data Deletion Requests

Users or server owners may request deletion of applicable information by contacting us using the details at the end of this Privacy Policy.

Please provide enough information for us to identify the relevant data, such as your Discord user ID and, where applicable, the relevant server ID.

We may need to verify the request before processing it.

Certain information may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, establishing or defending legal claims, or other legitimate purposes permitted by applicable law.

Some moderation information may also concern multiple parties or a Discord server's legitimate moderation records. Such requests will therefore be evaluated according to the circumstances and applicable law.

## 9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through the Service.

These measures may include access controls, restricted administrative functionality, environment-based secret management, authorization checks, and infrastructure security practices.

However, no internet-connected service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Server owners should grant Bot and dashboard permissions only to trusted personnel.

## 10. Bot Credentials and Secrets

Discord bot tokens, dashboard secrets, API credentials, database credentials, and similar sensitive values should be stored securely and should not be publicly disclosed.

If a credential is believed to have been exposed, it should be revoked or rotated as soon as reasonably possible.

## 11. International Processing

Depending on where the Service and its infrastructure providers are hosted, information may be processed in countries other than the country in which a user resides.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers of personal data.

## 12. Children's Privacy

Users must satisfy Discord's applicable age requirements.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected or processed in violation of applicable children's privacy requirements, we will take appropriate steps to address the situation.
